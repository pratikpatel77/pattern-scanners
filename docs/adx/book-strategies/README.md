# ADXcellence — entry and exit rules by strategy

Source: *ADXcellence: Power Trend Strategies*, Dr. Charles B. Schaap (2006). Page numbers are the book's printed pages. The book has **six** strategies.

| 2 | ADXtra | Enter the **breakout itself** | Break of the base trendline | [02-adxtra.md](02-adxtra.md) |

## Terms used in the rules

- **Pivot low reversal (up)**: price trades above the high of the pivot-low bar. This is the long entry. **Pivot high reversal (down)**: price trades below the low of the pivot-high bar. This is the short entry.
- **Crossover high**: the DMI crosses above the other DMI and makes a higher high than the other DMI's last peak, above 25.
- **Cross and hold**: the DMI crosses above, holds above the other DMI on a pullback, then makes a new high above 25.
- **Low-ADX base**: a consolidation of 30 or more bars with a price pattern and ADX under 25.

## Rules that apply to every strategy

- **Direction**: when +DMI is above −DMI, do not be short; when −DMI is above +DMI, do not be long.
- **Stop**: set before entry. Longs under the pivot low, shorts above the pivot high.
- **Trailing stop**: uptrends may be trailed two pivots back; downtrends are trailed above the last pivot high.
- **Change of dominance**: when the opposite DMI makes a crossover high, tighten the stop or exit.
- **Divergence** (price makes a new extreme but the dominant DMI does not) is a warning: take partial profit or tighten the stop.

## What the book does not give

- No profit targets, except the ADXtender notes.
- Chapter 18 ("ADXit") has **no exit rules**; it is about trader mindset.
- No position sizing, option strikes or expiries.
