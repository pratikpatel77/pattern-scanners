# 4. ADXtender

**Trend continuation**: after ADX rises above 25, enter every retracement to the 20 EMA. Can start a trade, join a trend, or add to a position. (Chapter 13, p.197–208)

## Long

### Entry
1. Price makes a **new high and ADX rises above 25.**
2. Price **retraces down near the 20 EMA.**
3. **ADX turns down** during the retracement.
4. **−DMI does not cross +DMI** (a slight cross without a change of dominance is acceptable; about 5 points of separation shows strength).
5. **Buy on a price pivot low reversal (up), with +DMI making a new high.**

Do not enter if:
- price made new highs but **ADX never got above 25** (p.199);
- there is **+DMI bearish divergence** (p.202);
- price broke the 20 EMA and the **−DMI made a cross and hold** (p.208).

If the entry fails because +DMI did not make a new high, look for a better entry in the **next 3 to 5 bars** (p.202).

### Exit
- **Stop**: under the pivot low, at the 20 EMA.
- **First target**: a test of the previous swing high.
- If price cannot clear the previous swing high: **take a small profit and exit**.
- Alternative: **sell half at the previous swing high**, let the other half ride.
- **Trail stops at all times.**
- **Add** on each later retracement to the 20 EMA.

## Short

### Entry
1. Price makes a **new low (under support) and ADX rises above 25.**
2. Price **retraces up near the 20 EMA.**
3. **ADX turns down** slightly during the retracement.
4. **+DMI does not cross −DMI** (or crosses slightly with no change of dominance), with good separation.
5. **Sell short on a price pivot high reversal (down), with −DMI making a new high.**

### Exit
- **Stop**: above the pivot high, near the 20 EMA.
- Targets and trailing as for the long side (previous swing low, half off, trail).
