# 6. ADXquisite

**Weekly + daily**: a weekly trend is intact, and the daily chart makes a correction that looks like a reversal but is not. Enter when daily dominance returns to the weekly direction. (Chapter 17, p.249–261)

## Long

### Entry
1. **Weekly +ADX peak of 25 or more** (on higher highs).
2. Price **retraces to the weekly 20 EMA** (slightly beyond is acceptable).
3. **Weekly −DMI does not cross weekly +DMI** on the retracement.
4. The **daily chart makes an ABC retracement** of about 20 to 40 bars (the C wave makes a lower low than the A wave).
5. **Daily +DMI makes a crossover high (or cross and hold)** while price makes a new high above resistance.
6. **Buy when price retraces to the daily 20 EMA, on a pivot low reversal (up).**

Do not rush the entry: wait for the daily signal (p.257).

### Exit
- **Stop**: under the pivot low at the 20 EMA.
- No separate exit rule in the book: **trail the stop**, and use the change-of-dominance rule. The book's S&P 500 example adds to the position twice after the first entry (p.261).

## Short

### Entry
1. **Weekly −ADX peak of 25 or more** (on lower lows).
2. Price **retraces up to the weekly 20 EMA.**
3. **Weekly +DMI does not cross weekly −DMI**, or crosses only slightly. Make sure −DMI is back on top before the daily entry.
4. The **daily chart makes an XYZ retracement** of about 20 to 40 bars.
5. **Daily −DMI makes a crossover high (or cross and hold)** while price makes a new low below support.
6. **Sell short when price retraces to the daily 20 EMA, on a pivot high reversal (down).**
   If the signal is a cross and hold with price at the 20 EMA, the entry may be **on the break of support** instead (p.259).

### Exit
- **Stop**: above the pivot high at the 20 EMA.
- **Trail** the stop above the last pivot high; exit on a change of dominance.
