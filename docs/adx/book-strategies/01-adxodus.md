# 1. ADXodus

Breakout from a long low-ADX base. Do not buy the breakout; enter the **first retracement to the 20 EMA**. (Chapter 10, p.171–178)

## Long

### Entry
1. A consolidation of **30 or more bars** with a price pattern.
2. ADX **under 25** during it, preferably under 15.
3. **Both DMI lines above ADX** before the breakout.
4. **+DMI makes a crossover high** as price breaks the top trendline.
5. Price **retraces to the 20 EMA and holds**, while **+DMI makes a pivot low at or near 25**.
6. **Buy on a price pivot low reversal (up).**

Do not enter if −DMI makes a new high but price does not make a lower low: price and DMI must agree (p.173).

### Exit
- **Stop**: under the pivot low.
- **Trail** the stop as the trend develops (two pivots back is allowed), or **add** to the position.
- **Exit if the −DMI makes a crossover high** (change of dominance, p.177).

## Short

### Entry
1. A consolidation of **30 or more bars** with a price pattern.
2. ADX **under 25**, preferably under 15.
3. **Both DMI lines above ADX** before the breakout.
4. **−DMI makes a crossover high** as price breaks the bottom trendline.
5. Price **retraces up to the 20 EMA and holds**, while **−DMI makes a pivot low at or near 25**.
6. **Sell short on a price pivot high reversal (down).**

### Exit
- **Stop**: above the pivot high.
- **Trail** the stop above the last pivot high.
- **Exit if the +DMI makes a crossover high.**

## Variation
An earlier entry is allowed at the **5 SMA** when the DMI makes a pivot low at 25 and price touches the 5 SMA (p.192).
