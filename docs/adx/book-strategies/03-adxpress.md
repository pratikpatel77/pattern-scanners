# 3. ADXpress

For **fast breakouts** that do not retrace to the 20 EMA: enter a short pause near the 5 or 10 SMA. (Chapter 12, p.187–195)

## Long

### Entry
1. Price makes a **20-day high**.
2. **+DMI makes a crossover high.**
3. **+DMI makes a pivot low above 25.**
4. **ADX above 15 and rising.**
5. Price pauses for **two to five bars near the 5 or 10 SMA** (narrow bars, inside bars or slightly lower lows).
6. **Buy on a price pivot low reversal (up).**

Do not enter if the +DMI pivot low is **under 25** or the bars after the breakout are narrow (p.193).

### Exit
- **Stop**: under the low of the pivot low.
- If the entry was a **gap up**, a tighter stop is allowed: under the 5 SMA at the gap level (p.190).
- No separate exit rule in the book: **trail the stop** and exit on a change of dominance.

## Short

### Entry
1. Price makes a **20-day low** on a wide-range bar.
2. **−DMI makes a crossover high.**
3. **−DMI makes a pivot low above 25.**
4. **ADX above 15 and rising.**
5. Price pauses for **two to five bars near the 5 or 10 SMA** (inside bars or slightly higher highs).
6. **Sell short on a price pivot high reversal (down).**

### Exit
- **Stop**: above the pivot high.
- **Trail** the stop above the last pivot high; exit on a change of dominance.

## Variation
If the daily chart has not paused, the same entry can be taken on a lower timeframe, such as the hourly 5 SMA (p.194–195).
