# 2. ADXtra

Breakout from a low-ADX base, entered **on the breakout itself**, not on a retracement. (Chapter 11, p.179–186)

## Long

### Entry
1. A consolidation of **30 or more bars** with a price pattern and trendlines.
2. ADX **under 25** during it, preferably under 15.
3. **Both DMI lines above ADX** before the breakout.
4. **+DMI makes a cross and hold** as price makes a high near the top trendline.
5. Price **retraces to or slightly under the 20 EMA**, while **+DMI makes a pivot low near 25**.
6. **Buy when price breaks above the upper trendline**, with **+DMI making a new high**.

Do not enter on a "crossover high" if price has not broken the trendline (p.183).

### Exit
- **Stop**: under the last pivot low.
- No separate exit rule in the book: **trail the stop** and exit on a change of dominance.

## Short

### Entry
1. A consolidation of **30 or more bars** with a price pattern and trendlines.
2. ADX **under 25** during it.
3. **Both DMI lines above ADX** before the breakout.
4. **−DMI makes a cross and hold** as price makes a low near the bottom trendline.
5. Price **retraces up near the 20 EMA**, while **−DMI makes a pivot low near 25**.
6. **Sell short when price breaks below the trendline**, with **−DMI making a new high**.

### Exit
- **Stop**: above the pivot high near the 20 EMA.
- **Trail** the stop above the last pivot high; exit on a change of dominance.
