# 5. ADXplosive

**Weekly + daily**: ADX is low on both timeframes; enter the **daily retracement** after the daily breakout. The weekly is the governing timeframe. (Chapter 16, p.235–248)

## Long

### Entry
1. **Weekly ADX under 25.**
2. **Daily ADX under 25**, with a daily consolidation of **20 or more bars**.
3. Price **breaks above daily resistance and daily ADX rises above 25**, with a valid **+DMI crossover high**.
4. Price **retraces down to the daily 20 EMA.**
5. **Weekly +DMI is above (or has crossed above) weekly −DMI** by the time price reaches the daily 20 EMA.
6. **Buy on a daily pivot low reversal (up).**

Do not enter on a DMI new high with no new price high: wait for a valid crossover high (p.240).

### Exit
- **Stop**: under the pivot low, near the daily 20 EMA.
- **Trail** the stop up once the trend is established, and **add** on later pullbacks (p.239).

## Short

### Entry
1. **Weekly ADX under 25.**
2. **Daily ADX under 25**, with a daily consolidation of **20 or more bars**.
3. Price **breaks below daily support and daily ADX rises above 25**, with a valid **−DMI crossover high**.
4. Price **retraces up to the daily 20 EMA.**
5. **Weekly −DMI is above (or has crossed above) weekly +DMI** by the time price reaches the daily 20 EMA.
6. **Sell short on a daily pivot high reversal (down).**

### Exit
- **Stop**: above the pivot high, near the daily 20 EMA.
- **Trail** the stop above the last pivot high.

## Variation
An earlier entry is allowed using the ADXpress pause at the 5 SMA on a fast mover (p.241).
