What the probabilities mean

Kronos-small uses 32 sampled paths. The breakout figure is the share of sampled paths that confirm above the detected pivot and reach the pattern target before invalidation. It is not a calibrated probability of a real breakout. The model uses 128 historical bars with fixed sampling settings.

The shaded range covers the 10th–90th percentiles of sampled prices. Check its actual coverage and price error in Backtest before relying on it. Saved results retain their original settings.
