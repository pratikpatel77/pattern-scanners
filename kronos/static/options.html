How the desk calculates

Strikes are compared against the 10th percentile of path lows and 90th percentile of path highs. These are estimated zones, not hard price limits. The app compares credit/debit spreads, condors, iron butterflies, butterflies, broken wings and 1×2 backspreads with bounded loss.

Ranking uses modelled outcomes at the holding date, with IV unchanged, 25% lower and 25% higher. Black–Scholes values are estimates, not future executable quotes. Scenario profit share is not proven win rate.

Costs: zero brokerage; STT 0.15% on sell premium; exchange 0.03553%; SEBI 0.0001%; GST 18% on exchange and SEBI; stamp 0.003% on buys. DP is zero for closing option trades. Figures are estimates, with an exit reserve in expiry charts. Paper closes use actual simulated turnover. No expiry settlement, delivery fees or margin funding is simulated.

Plans use one lot. Planned loss is not broker margin. Unscheduled events and stock correlations are not modelled; the total open risk limit is conservative and gives no diversification credit. Complex multi-leg fills may be harder to execute.

Forecasts are saved whether a trade is taken or skipped. No backtesting and no broker orders.
